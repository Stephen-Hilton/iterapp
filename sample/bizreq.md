# Business requirements: greet

The sample product is `greet`, a tiny command-line greeter used to demo iterloop
end-to-end.

- BR-1: Given a name, greet greets that person by name.
- BR-2: Given no name, greet greets the world.
- BR-3: Greetings are friendly and end with an exclamation mark.
- BR-4 (future): greet supports a `--shout` flag that uppercases the greeting.
- BR-5 (future): greet supports localized greetings via a `--lang` flag.
